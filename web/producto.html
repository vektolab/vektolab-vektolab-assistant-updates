How membership download works

Vektolab makes its designs available to the community through Patreon. Below is the full process, step by step.

1

Choose a membership tier

Go to the Vektolab Patreon page and select the tier that best fits your interests.

2

Confirm the subscription

Patreon processes the payment and activates membership automatically, with no extra steps on your side.

3

Connect your account with Vektolab

When you sign in with Patreon on the site, the system can verify active membership each time a download is requested.

4

Download the designs

Once membership is verified, the STL file is available for immediate download at no extra cost.

5

Renewal and cancellation

Membership renews automatically according to the chosen cycle. You can cancel anytime from your Patreon account.

When the listing allows it, you can also buy that download individually via PayPal, without subscribing.
